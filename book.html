The Book & News

Mary's memoir of her life with Arnold, and the gallery in the news.

Mind and Matter: Life with Arnold Mikelson

By Mary Mikelson, with writer Barbara Gould.

Mary's memoir of their twenty-two years together, from Arnold's beginnings in Latvia and post-war England to Edmonton and British Columbia. It shows the artist, the family man and his humour, in his own words: Mary kept Arnold's way of speaking just as it was.

The book was launched at the gallery on May 2, 2015. Copies are available at the gallery.
