The Gallery

A 2,000-square-foot A-frame gallery on three acres of gardens, built by Arnold Mikelson and still welcoming visitors every day.

What you'll find

The largest collection of Arnold Mikelson's wood sculptures, shown where he made them.

Original work by local artists, all for sale: paintings in oil, acrylic and watercolour, pottery, ceramics and raku, blown and fused glass, soapstone carving, fabric art and woodturning.

And outside, three acres of gardens under old cedar trees, worth a slow walk before or after your visit.

Forty years of the Festival of Arts

Arnold and Mary started the Arnold Mikelson Festival of Arts at the gallery in 1973. Artists, musicians and neighbours gathered on the grounds every summer for forty years. The fortieth and final festival was held in July 2013.
