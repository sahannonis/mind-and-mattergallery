Welcome to the gallery

Arnold Mikelson built this A-frame gallery with his own hands and opened its doors in 1966.

Sixty years later, it still sits on three acres of gardens under the old cedars on 16th Avenue. Inside you'll find the largest collection of Arnold's wood sculptures, alongside original paintings, pottery, glass, ceramics and soapstone by local artists, all for sale.

His wife Mary has kept the doors open ever since, and she's often there to show visitors around.

Sixty years in a few dates

  1. 1922

    Arnold Mikelson is born in Rauna, Latvia, the son of a cabinetmaker.

  2. 1966

    The Mind & Matter Gallery opens on 16th Avenue in South Surrey.

  3. 1973

    The Arnold Mikelson Festival of Arts begins. It runs for forty years.

  4. 2015

    Mary publishes her memoir, Mind and Matter: Life with Arnold Mikelson.

  5. 2026

    The gallery celebrates sixty years.
